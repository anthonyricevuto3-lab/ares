# Inspection report template

Do not file this template as a completed NASA Formal Inspection. Leave names blank until people are assigned. Do not invent inspectors.

NASA-STD-8739.9 section 8.1 is the source of the measurement fields. Severity uses both that standard's Major / Minor / Clerical classes and the ARES Critical / High / Medium / Low scale. They are recorded side by side because they are not interchangeable.
